Aude Sustainable Studio: Crafting an Award-Winning Luxury Storefront

How Valence combined editorial fashion aesthetics with high-performance headless architecture to elevate online checkout conversion by +41%.

Client
Aude Studio Stockholm
Industry
Luxury Fashion & Sustainable Goods
Timeline
5 Weeks (Design & Launch)
Services Delivered
E-commerce, UI/UX Systems, CRO

The Challenge

Aude Studio is a high-end Scandinavian fashion house specializing in sustainably sourced cashmere, tailored outerwear, and handcrafted leather accessories. While their retail flagship in Stockholm was thriving, their existing online storefront suffered from slow loading images, an antiquated checkout flow, and high cart abandonment rates exceeding 78%.

Their target audience expects an immaculate editorial experience on mobile devices — indistinguishable from browsing an art monograph. Slow page loads and clunky multi-step checkouts were eroding customer trust and revenue.

The Strategic Solution

Valence created a bespoke editorial e-commerce layout that marries minimalist luxury typography with an ultra-responsive product grid. We implemented lazy-loaded, modern WebP imagery with blur-up placeholders to guarantee instant visual rendering without layout shifts.

To cure cart abandonment, we engineered a frictionless slide-out shopping bag that calculates shipping, taxes, and applied discount vouchers in real time. We integrated native 1-touch Apple Pay and Google Pay checkouts through Stripe Elements, reducing checkout friction to less than 15 seconds.
