Aura Wealth Management: Modernizing High-Net-Worth Portfolio Telemetry

How Valence engineered a high-security, sub-second wealth management web portal that transformed client portfolio interactions and won executive praise.

Client
Aura Wealth Capital
Industry
Fintech & Private Equity
Timeline
6 Weeks (Discovery to Launch)
Services Delivered
Web Application, UI/UX, API Integration

The Challenge

Aura Wealth manages private portfolios for high-net-worth investors across North America and Europe. Their previous client portal suffered from slow response times (often exceeding 4.2 seconds per page load), cluttered tabular data, and an obsolete legacy styling system that failed to communicate modern financial authority.

Client onboarding drop-offs were increasing, and mobile usage was virtually unusable due to rigid desktop-only data tables. The leadership team needed a complete overhaul: lightning-fast page transitions, an intuitive mobile interface, and rigorous compliance with financial security standards.

The Strategic Solution

Valence partnered directly with Aura's CPO and engineering leadership. We completely redesigned the client experience from scratch in Figma, adopting a sophisticated dark slate aesthetic with high-contrast glowing telemetry charts and legible typography.

On the frontend, we engineered a custom, zero-dependency modern JavaScript architecture with virtualized scrolling for large transaction histories. Every component was built responsive from the ground up, allowing portfolio managers and clients to monitor positions seamlessly on mobile smartphones, tablets, and 4K displays.

Key Highlights & Features

  • Instant search and filtering across thousands of historical transaction ledger rows
