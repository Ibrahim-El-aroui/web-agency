Our Narrative

Why We Founded Valence

The digital landscape is overrun with generic templates, fragile plugins, and bloated agency promises. Too many businesses find themselves trapped with slow websites that alienate users, break under updates, and fail to generate qualified leads.

Valence was established to offer an authentic alternative. We approach web development as a refined discipline — combining creative visual design with rigorous software engineering principles.

Every layout we design has an intentional hierarchy. Every line of code is written with semantic clarity. And every project is delivered with the goal of driving measurable, long-term business growth.

Core Methodology

The Principles That Guide Every Build

These four non-negotiable standards govern our design decisions, engineering architecture, and client partnerships.

01 / INTEGRITY

Craft Over Bloat

We never patch together sluggish, multipurpose pre-made themes. We handcraft custom websites with clean markup, modular styles, and optimized assets that execute instantaneously.

02 / TRANSPARENCY

Direct Senior Access

You collaborate directly with the senior designers and engineers executing your project. No layers of non-technical account executives or miscommunicated design feedback.

03 / INCLUSIVITY

Accessibility as Standard

We design and code to WCAG 2.1 AA standards by default. Semantic HTML landmarks, full keyboard navigability, high color contrast, and assistive tech compatibility.

04 / AUTONOMY

Zero Vendor Lock-in

When the project finishes, you receive full ownership of all repositories, Figma source files, documentation, and credentials. You have total freedom to manage your website however you choose.
