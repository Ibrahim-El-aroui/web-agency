HealWell Care: Intuitive Healthcare & Telehealth Architecture

Building an accessible, HIPAA-conscious medical appointment portal and teleconsultation platform engineered for patient ease and high clinician adoption.

Client
HealWell Health Technologies
Industry
Healthtech & Medical Portals
Timeline
5 Weeks
Services Delivered
Web Platform, UI/UX Design, WCAG 2.1 AA

The Challenge

HealWell operates a nationwide network of medical practitioners and telehealth doctors. Their original appointment portal suffered from steep booking drop-offs, difficult mobile navigation for elderly patients, and lack of WCAG accessibility compliance. Patients frequently abandoned digital bookings and dialed call centers instead, increasing clinic overhead costs.

The Strategic Solution

Valence redesigned the entire patient journey around high-contrast visual cues, large touch targets, and a 3-step appointment booking wizard. We engineered an accessible appointment scheduling interface with doctor specialty filters, real-time slot calendar synchronization, and automated SMS reminders.

Verified Performance & Business Results

+93%
Increase in Completed Online Bookings
100%
WCAG 2.1 AA Accessibility Pass
0.6s
Fast Mobile Consultation Load Time

Key Highlights & Architecture

  • Frictionless 3-step appointment booking calendar with timezone autodetection
  • High-contrast readable typography and screen-reader compliant aria-roles
  • Doctor directory with verified credentials, patient reviews, and languages spoken
  • Secure encrypted patient intake questionnaire and direct integration with clinic EHRs
